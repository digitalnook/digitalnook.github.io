Privacy Policy – DigitalNookDK Pinterest Publisher

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This app is a private tool used only by the owner of the Etsy shop DigitalNookDK to publish Pins of the shop's own products to the shop's own Pinterest account.

What data the app accesses

  • The shop owner's own Pinterest account: boards and Pins, used only to create Pins of the shop's own listings and to read basic Pin statistics.
  • No data about other Pinterest users is requested, collected or stored.

How data is stored

  • Pinterest access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a file readable only by the owner, and are never shared with anyone.
  • No personal data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising profiles.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are deleted when access is revoked. Access can be revoked at any time in Pinterest settings under "Apps".
